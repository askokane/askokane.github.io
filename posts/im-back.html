im back

2026.11.20 · 2 min read

it has been more than three months from my last blog post and i have not posted here in a bit. yeah, it has been a busy semester of university. this computer organization class is actually killing me and i never knew that multivariable calculus would go into neural fricking networks. the senate has kept me busy too. working on three different resolutions now and a fourth one was just proposed today and i know soon enough we will reach five.

so, why have i not posted here in a while? i honestly don't know. i think i just don't have a reason to post here, at least that is what i think. but, i do think that documenting my myriad of stupid, interesting, quirky, curious and confusing thoughts here might end up being useful in the future, and it actually might. so, here i am again. back to posting on my blog.

i also have thought of publishing my class notes on my website as a resource. it may intrigue fellow academics to learn from. i know i looked into websites that had notes or material on anything that i found interesting, which was mostly mathematics and computer science. so, yeah, i will hopefully soon enough in the winter add a tab for my class notes. i am also taking introductory psychology in the winter, so i might take notes for that class.

i think that is all i had to say honestly. i want to start posting more blogs, but i am kind of in that writing block kind of head space at the moment. i need some inspiration. maybe new york might help with that. oh yeah, i am headed to new york day after tomorrow for thanksgiving break for 6 days. might help with some inspiration.
