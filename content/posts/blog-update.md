alright so i have been writing blogs for over 2 years now, it is just that i did not migrate my old blog posts from my old website and i definitely will be migrating all my blog posts from here on out, provided that i do change my website. for now, i think my website is designed well enough (even though it does not fully reflect my taste), for now. but, i feel like my blog posts could do with some work.

i am trying to kind of scope out what readers would like to read and see on my blogs. i tend to avoid longer blog posts, just because i know some people are not readers and do not like reading a lengthy blog about something in life. i, myself, like shorter posts that can communicate information within a few 100 words, so i would like to continue with that format but the structure is what i want to improve.

nowadays, i post barely a blog per month and there is no real structure to when i put a post out. i want to kind of consolidate a few ideas for blog posts for each month or for each week. now, i know that i am not a writer and cannot sit behind a screen typing and editing blogs every single week. so yeah, i think i will stick to that monthly format. sort of like a monthly reflection on what happened that month and what does the next month look like for me. i also think i am going to do a list of media that changed my life every month.

why do i think this is important? well, for one i just wanted to put something out today, that is also a reason i decided to write this. but, i honestly also think that i will be starting a lot of new things this coming semester of university and i think this blog could help me vent, cope, celebrate, observe new moments as they come. you will also get a lot of updates from this month's reflection that will then help you connect the dots. actually that is what i should get into next.

## July 2026

so, what exactly did happen this month? well i went to co/build a lot. i spoke to a lot of cool people on what they were building and got valuable advice on what i should be doing to get into the builder space and grow myself in the space. i also felt a hint of imposter syndrome hit me after i left every co/build session. but i think that's exactly where i found my footing. after a few hard weeks of not understanding what i should be doing a few ideas for me really hit it off.

most of this month i was honestly lost. i annoyed a friend that i met at co/build about it a bunch. she really helped me get out of my slump. i was so lost on where to even begin. i had no ideas, no projects open, nothing to work on. this was my lowest point in a while. i felt the burden of moving forward in life while doing nothing. i was completely burnt out.

something that i did not consider was my own health and mental when i was in this phase. i was not eating and trying to just find ideas. i was on youtube or x at 3 am trying to find ideas, losing my sleep. then i talked to my parents about it and they just said relax. i thought they were just telling me that because i am their son, but then i thought about it. even if i took their advice and took a break for a bit, it is my vacation. i honestly could take a break and nothing would happen except i would fall behind the competition for a bit. so i took their advice and enjoyed myself with a waterpark trip, food and hella movies (i am not kidding when i say hella, check my [letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/atharva_kokane/diary/) out).

it took a few days, but it finally clicked. i was thinking about university again and realized virginia tech doesn't really have a builder space. no room where people who make things end up in the same place. that's the gap, and that's exactly what i want to build for my community back at tech. so i didn't wait. got on a call with a few talented builders from tech, and now it's two weeks until i'm back in blacksburg to keep going with them. the one question we couldn't answer: quality control. how do you keep it real without keeping it closed. there has to be a solution, but that is something to tackle when i get there. this is also something i am going to closely guard though.

i was also invited by my friend to a startup based in italy. now, i don't want to say much about it, but it is going great. we have an idea of where this thing could go and i also finished building the mvp. we also have a cracked team and everyone complements everyone so well. thank you to [massimo giglio](https://www.linkedin.com/in/massimo-giglio-aa67463a8/) and [sofia grassi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sofiagrassi/) for trusting me with my skills and for bringing such talented people together. that sounded like linkedin talk for a bit.
